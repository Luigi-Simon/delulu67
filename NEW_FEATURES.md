# New Features Added - Study Battle MVP

## 🎁 Chest Reward System

### Overview
When a match ends, all players receive chest rewards based on their final placement and HP ranking.

### Chest Types

#### 📦 Bronze Chest
- **Who gets it**: 4th place and below
- **Cards**: 2 cards
- **Drop rates**: 80% Common, 18% Rare, 2% Epic
- **Guaranteed**: None

#### 🎁 Silver Chest
- **Who gets it**: 2nd-3rd place (or 2nd in duo)
- **Cards**: 3 cards
- **Drop rates**: 60% Common, 35% Rare, 5% Epic
- **Guaranteed**: 1 Rare card minimum

#### 💎 Gold Chest
- **Who gets it**: 1st place in duo, or 2nd in 4+ player match
- **Cards**: 4 cards
- **Drop rates**: 40% Common, 45% Rare, 15% Epic
- **Guaranteed**: 1 Rare card minimum

#### 👑 Diamond Chest
- **Who gets it**: 1st place in 4+ player match
- **Cards**: 5 cards
- **Drop rates**: 20% Common, 50% Rare, 30% Epic
- **Guaranteed**: 1 Epic card minimum

### How It Works

1. **End Match Button**: Any player can click "🏁 End Match" in the active match panel
2. **Confirmation**: A popup asks for confirmation
3. **Ranking**: Players are ranked by HP (highest to lowest)
4. **Chest Distribution**: Each player receives a chest based on placement
5. **Card Addition**: Cards are automatically added to:
   - Collection (all cards)
   - Hand (if space available, up to 3 cards)
6. **Reward Display**: A beautiful animated popup shows your chest and cards

### Example Scenarios

**1v1 Duel:**
- Winner (1st): Gold Chest 💎
- Loser (2nd): Silver Chest 🎁

**4-Player Group:**
- 1st place: Diamond Chest 👑
- 2nd place: Gold Chest 💎
- 3rd place: Silver Chest 🎁
- 4th place: Bronze Chest 📦

## 😎 Emote System

### Overview
Taunt, encourage, or flex on your opponents during matches with fun emotes that appear in the activity feed!

### How to Use

1. Click the **"😎 Emotes"** button in the active match panel
2. Choose from 14 different emotes across 3 categories
3. Your emote appears in the activity feed for all players to see
4. Emotes are visible in real-time to all match participants

### Emote Categories

#### 🔥 Taunts (6 emotes)
Perfect for psychological warfare!

- **"67 67 67"** 🔥 - The ultimate flex for long study sessions
- **"Haha you're not studying enough!"** 😂 - Classic taunt
- **"Stop slacking!"** 😴 - Call out lazy opponents
- **"This is too easy!"** 😎 - Show your dominance
- **"Is that all you got?"** 💪 - Challenge their strength
- **"Focus harder!"** 🎯 - Demand more from opponents

#### 💪 Flex (5 emotes)
Show off your achievements!

- **"I'm on fire! 🔥🔥🔥"** 🔥 - You're unstoppable
- **"Unstoppable!"** ⚡ - Nothing can stop you
- **"Just got an EPIC card!"** ✨ - Brag about your luck
- **"Grinding hard!"** 💯 - Show your dedication
- **"Study beast mode activated!"** 🦁 - Maximum effort

#### 👍 Encourage (3 emotes)
Be a good sport!

- **"Good luck!"** 🍀 - Wish opponents well
- **"Nice try!"** 👍 - Acknowledge effort
- **"Well played!"** 👏 - Show respect

### Emote Display

Emotes appear in the activity feed like this:
```
😂 JohnDoe: "Haha you're not studying enough!"
🔥 JaneSmith: "67 67 67"
👍 AlexChen: "Well played!"
```

### Strategic Use

- **Before match**: Send "Good luck!" to be friendly
- **During focus session**: Taunt with "67 67 67" to show commitment
- **After big play**: Flex with "Unstoppable!"
- **When winning**: Taunt with "This is too easy!"
- **When losing**: Encourage with "Nice try!" to stay positive

## 🎮 Updated UI Features

### Match Panel Enhancements

1. **Three Action Buttons**:
   - **😎 Emotes**: Open emote selector
   - **📋 Copy ID**: Copy match ID to clipboard

   - Shows last 10 actions
   - Emotes appear with emoji and player name
   - Card plays show damage/healing amounts

### Chest Reward Modal

- **Animated entrance**: Bounces in for dramatic effect
- **Beautiful gradient**: Yellow-to-orange gradient background
- **Large chest emoji**: Shows chest type clearly
- **Card list**: All received cards with rarity colors
- **Rarity indicators**: Epic (purple), Rare (blue), Common (gray)

### Emote Modal

- **Categorized layout**: Organized by Taunts, Flex, Encourage
- **Color-coded**: Red for taunts, blue for flex, green for encourage
- **Hover effects**: Buttons light up on hover
- **Quick close**: X button or click outside to close
